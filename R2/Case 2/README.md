# MATLAB Simulation Model - Case 2

This repository contains the MATLAB code and data used to produce *case 2* results in the research article **"New probabilistic multi-criteria model for assessing the integral sustainability of private cars with different propulsion systems"** Cartelle Barros, J.J., Cardenal, J., de la Cruz López, M.P., del Caño Gochi, A. (2025) that is currently is under review for publication in the *Journal of Cleaner Production*


## General description

It contains four subdirectories, each providing the MATLAB functions used to perform the Monte Carlo simulations that estimate integral and partial sustainability indices for conventional, hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and battery-electric vehicles. The simulations implement the procedures defined in the four environmental sub-models described in the second revision of the paper.

`1.GreenNCAP`: Matlab code used to obtain integral and partial sustainability indices estimations for conventional, hybrid, plug-in hybrid and battery electrical vehicules according to Green NCAP Rating Procedure 2022 (https://www.greenncap.com/wp-content/uploads/Rating_Procedure_2022_v3.0.0.pdf) environmental sub-model as they are presented in the second revision of the paper.
`2.BicerDincer`: Matlab code used to obtain integral and partial sustainability indices estimations for conventional, hybrid, plug-in hybrid and battery electrical vehicules according to Bicer and Dincer (doi:10.1016/j.resconrec.2018.01.036) environmental sub-model as they are presented in the second revision of the paper.
`3.Petrauskine`: Matlab code used to obtain integral and partial sustainability indices estimations for conventional, hybrid, plug-in hybrid and battery electrical vehicules according to Petrauskienė, et al. (doi:10.3390/su13020957) environmental sub-model as they are presented in the second revision of the paper.
`4.Naranjo`: Matlab code used to obtain integral and partial sustainability indices estimations for conventional, hybrid, plug-in hybrid and battery electrical vehicules according to of Puig-Samper Naranjo, et al. (doi:10.1016/j.jclepro.2021.125883) environmental sub-model as they are presented in the second revision of the paper.
